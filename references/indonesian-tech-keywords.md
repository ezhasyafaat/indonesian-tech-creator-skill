## Hashtag Research Tips

### Find Trending Hashtags
1. Check TikTok Discover page
2. Look at Instagram Explore
3. Search Twitter trending topics
4. Check YouTube trending in Indonesia
5. Look at what top Indonesian tech creators use

### Hashtag Tools
- TikTok: Search bar autocomplete
- Instagram: Hashtag suggestions when typing
- Twitter: Trending section
- YouTube: Search suggestions
- Google Trends: Compare keyword popularity

### Hashtag Strategy
- Mix popular (1M+ posts) + medium (100K-1M) + niche (<100K)
- Rotate hashtags (don't use same ones every post)
- Track which hashtags perform best
- Remove underperforming hashtags
- Add trending hashtags when relevant
