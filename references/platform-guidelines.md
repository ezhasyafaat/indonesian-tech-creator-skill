# Platform Guidelines

## TikTok

### Content Specs
- **Video length**: 15-60 seconds (sweet spot: 30-45 detik)
- **Aspect ratio**: 9:16 vertical (1080x1920)
- **File format**: MP4 or MOV
- **Max file size**: 287.6 MB
- **Caption**: Up to 2200 characters

### Best Practices
- Hook di 3 detik pertama
- Pakai trending sounds/music
- Text overlay besar dan jelas
- CTA yang jelas (follow, save, comment)
- Post konsisten (daily atau 5x/week)

### Algorithm Tips
- Engagement di 2 jam pertama = critical
- Reply semua comments
- Share ke other platforms (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts)
- Pakai 3-5 hashtags relevan
- Post di peak hours: 19:00-21:00 WIB

### Don'ts
- Jangan pakai watermark dari platform lain
- Jangan upload video horizontal
- Jangan caption terlalu panjang (orang males baca)
- Jangan post tanpa hook yang kuat

## YouTube

### Content Specs
- **Video length**: 5-15 minutes (sweet spot: 8-12 menit untuk tutorial)
- **Aspect ratio**: 16:9 horizontal (1920x1080 minimum)
- **File format**: MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **Max file size**: 256 GB
- **Title**: Up to 100 characters
- **Description**: Up to 5000 characters

### Best Practices
- Hook kuat di 30 detik pertama
- Intro singkat (5-10 detik) langsung ke content
- Chapters/timestamps di description
- End screen dengan subscribe + next video
- Cards di timestamps penting
- Thumbnail yang eye-catching

### Algorithm Tips
- Watch time = most important metric
- CTR (click-through rate) dari thumbnail + title
- Engagement: likes, comments, shares
- Consistency: 2x/week minimum
- SEO: keywords di title, description, tags

### Don'ts
- Jangan intro terlalu panjang (max 10 detik)
- Jangan title clickbait yang nggak deliver
- Jangan thumbnail yang misleading
- Jangan upload tanpa description yang proper

## Instagram

### Reels
- **Video length**: 15-90 seconds (sweet spot: 30-60 detik)
- **Aspect ratio**: 9:16 vertical (1080x1920)
- **Caption**: Up to 2200 characters
- **Hashtags**: Up to 30 (sweet spot: 15-20)

### Carousels
- **Slides**: Up to 10 images/videos
- **Aspect ratio**: 1:1 square (1080x1080) or 4:5 portrait (1080x1350)
- **Caption**: Up to 2200 characters

### Stories
- **Duration**: 15 seconds per slide
- **Max slides**: Unlimited (but 10 is good practice)
- **Interactive elements**: Polls, quizzes, questions, sliders

### Feed Posts
- **Image**: 1:1 square (1080x1080) or 4:5 portrait (1080x1350)
- **Video**: Up to 60 seconds
- **Caption**: Up to 2200 characters

### Best Practices
- Consistent visual branding (colors, fonts, style)
- Post Reels daily atau 5x/week
- Post feed 3-5x/week
- Stories daily
- Pakai interactive stickers di Stories
- Cross-post dari TikTok (tapi remove watermark)

### Algorithm Tips
- Saves dan shares = high value
- Engagement di 1 jam pertama = critical
- Consistency > frequency
- Hashtag research penting
- Post di peak hours: 12:00-13:00, 19:00-21:00 WIB

### Don'ts
- Jangan post blurry images
- Jangan caption tanpa line breaks (hard to read)
- Jangan pakai terlalu banyak hashtags (#spam)
- Jangan ignore comments

## Blog

### Content Specs
- **Word count**: 1000-2500 words (sweet spot: 1500 words)
- **Headings**: H1 (title), H2 (sections), H3 (subsections)
- **Images**: 1 featured image + 1 per 300-500 words
- **Code blocks**: Syntax highlighted, copyable

### SEO Best Practices
- Title tag: 50-60 characters, primary keyword
- Meta description: 150-160 characters
- URL slug: Short, keyword-rich
- H1: One per page, includes primary keyword
- H2s: Include secondary keywords
- Internal links: 3-5 per article
- External links: 2-3 authoritative sources
- Image alt text: Descriptive, includes keywords

### Content Structure
```
# Title (H1)
Intro (hook + what they'll learn)

## Section 1 (H2)
Content with code examples

## Section 2 (H2)
Content with screenshots

## Section 3 (H2)
Content with diagrams

## Conclusion (H2)
Summary + next steps + CTA
```

### Best Practices
- Hook di 2 kalimat pertama
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Bullet points dan numbered lists
- Code examples yang real (bukan toy examples)
- Screenshots setiap 300-500 words
- Internal links ke related articles
- CTA di akhir (comment, share, subscribe)

### Don'ts
- Jangan wall of text (break up dengan visuals)
- Jangan keyword stuffing
- Jangan copy-paste content dari tempat lain
- Jangan publish tanpa proofreading

## Twitter/X

### Content Specs
- **Tweet**: Up to 280 characters
- **Thread**: Up to 25 tweets
- **Image**: 16:9 landscape (1600x900) or 1:1 square
- **Video**: Up to 2:20 minutes

### Best Practices
- Hook di tweet pertama (buat orang klik "Show this thread")
- One idea per tweet
- Number tweets (1/, 2/, 3/)
- Visual di setiap tweet kalau bisa
- CTA di tweet terakhir (follow, retweet, comment)
- Post threads 2-3x/week

### Algorithm Tips
- Engagement di 1 jam pertama = critical
- Replies > retweets > likes
- Consistency: daily tweets
- Engage dengan other accounts
- Post di peak hours: 08:00-09:00, 12:00-13:00, 19:00-21:00 WIB

### Don'ts
- Jangan tweet tanpa hook
- Jangan thread yang terlalu panjang (max 15 tweets)
- Jangan spam hashtags
- Jangan ignore replies

## Cross-Platform Strategy

### Repurposing Workflow
1. **YouTube video** → Cut into TikTok/Reels clips
2. **Blog post** → Convert to Twitter thread
3. **TikTok** → Repost as Instagram Reel (remove watermark)
4. **Instagram carousel** → Convert to blog post
5. **All content** → Share to Stories

### Platform-Specific Adjustments
- **TikTok → Instagram**: Remove watermark, adjust caption length
- **YouTube → TikTok**: Cut best moments, add text overlay
- **Blog → Twitter**: Extract key points, make thread
- **Instagram → Blog**: Expand carousel points into full article

### Consistency Tips
- Same visual branding across platforms
- Same posting schedule (adjust for platform peak times)
- Cross-promote (link to other platforms)
- Repurpose, don't recreate (save time)
