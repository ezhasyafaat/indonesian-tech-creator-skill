## Platform Monetization

### TikTok Creator Fund
- **Requirements**: 10K+ followers, 100K views in last 30 days, 18+ years old
- **Payment**: $0.02-0.04 per 1000 views (varies)
- **Estimated**: Rp 200-500K/bulan for 10K followers
- **How to apply**: TikTok Creator Portal
- **Notes**: Payment varies by region, Indonesia rates lower than US

### YouTube AdSense
- **Requirements**: 1K subscribers + 4K watch hours (last 12 months)
- **Payment**: $1-5 per 1000 views (CPM varies by niche)
- **Estimated**: Rp 300K-1K/bulan for 5K subscribers
- **How to apply**: YouTube Partner Program
- **Notes**: Tech niche has decent CPM, Indonesian audience = lower CPM than US

### Instagram Bonuses
- **Requirements**: Invite-only, high engagement
- **Payment**: Varies (up to $10K/bulan for top creators)
- **Estimated**: Rp 500K-5K/bulan if eligible
- **How to apply**: Wait for invite or check Professional Dashboard
- **Notes**: Focus on Reels performance

### Facebook Stars
- **Requirements**: 500+ followers for 30 days
- **Payment**: $0.01 per star
- **Estimated**: Rp 100-500K/bulan
- **How to apply**: Enable in Professional Dashboard
- **Notes**: Good if cross-posting to Facebook

## Payment Methods (Indonesia)

### Receiving Payments
- **Bank Transfer**: BCA, Mandiri, BNI, BRI (most common)
- **E-wallet**: GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay
- **PayPal**: For international payments
- **Wise**: Lower fees for international

### Tax Considerations
- **PPh 21**: Income tax for individuals (progressive rates)
- **PPh Final**: For certain income types (e.g., rental)
- **PPN**: VAT 11% if revenue > 4.8M/bulan
- **Notes**: Keep records, consult tax professional

## Tips for Success

1. **Diversify income**: Don't rely on one source
2. **Track everything**: Use spreadsheet to monitor revenue
3. **Disclose sponsorships**: Follow FTC guidelines + Indonesian regulations
4. **Negotiate rates**: Don't accept first offer
5. **Long-term partnerships**: Better than one-off sponsorships
6. **Quality over quantity**: Don't over-monetize and lose trust
7. **Reinvest**: Use revenue to improve content quality
