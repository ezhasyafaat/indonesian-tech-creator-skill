# Instagram Content Template

## Quick Reference
- **Reels**: 15-90 seconds (sweet spot: 30-60 detik)
- **Stories**: 15 seconds per slide, max 10 slides
- **Feed posts**: Single image, carousel (up to 10 slides), or video
- **Captions**: Up to 2200 characters, first 125 chars most important
- **Aspect ratios**: Reels 9:16, Feed 1:1 or 4:5, Stories 9:16

## Instagram Strategy for Indonesian Tech Creators

### Best Posting Times (WIB)
- **Reels**: 19:00-21:00 (peak), 12:00-13:00 (lunch)
- **Stories**: Throughout day, peak 19:00-21:00
- **Feed posts**: 12:00-13:00, 19:00-21:00
- **Carousels**: 12:00-13:00 (people save for later)

### Hashtag Strategy
**Mix 15-20 hashtags:**
- 5 Indonesian: #DeveloperIndonesia #TechIndonesia #ProgrammingIndonesia
- 5 English: #WebDev #JavaScript #Coding #Developer #Tech
- 5 Niche: #VSCode #Git #React #NodeJS #Python
- 3-5 Trending: Check Instagram trending

### Content Mix
- **40% Educational**: Tutorials, tips, how-to
- **30% Engagement**: Polls, questions, Q&A
- **20% Behind-the-scenes**: Setup, workflow, day in life
- **10% Promotional**: Products, services, collaborations

### Engagement Tactics
- Reply to all comments within 2 hours
- Use interactive stickers in Stories (polls, quizzes, questions)
- Ask questions in captions
- Engage with similar accounts (comment on their posts)
- Use "Close Friends" for exclusive content
- Create Highlights for evergreen content

### Growth Hacks
- Collaborate with other tech creators (collab posts)
- Use trending audio in Reels
- Post consistently (daily Reels, 3-5 feed posts/week)
- Cross-promote from TikTok (remove watermark)
- Share user-generated content (with permission)
- Use Instagram Live for Q&A sessions
