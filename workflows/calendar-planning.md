# Content Calendar Planning Workflow

## Overview
Create 2-4 week content calendars with balanced content mix, optimal posting times, and cross-platform coordination for Indonesian tech creators.

## Process

### Step 1: Gather Requirements
Ask user for:
- **Platforms**: TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Blog, Twitter
- **Posting frequency**: How often per platform
- **Niche**: Web dev, mobile, data science, etc.
- **Duration**: 2 weeks, 4 weeks, 1 month
- **Content pillars**: 3-5 main topics/themes
- **Goals**: Growth, engagement, monetization

### Step 2: Define Content Pillars

**Content pillars** are main themes you consistently create content about.

**Example for web dev niche:**
1. **Tutorials** (40%) — Step-by-step guides, how-tos
2. **Tips & Tricks** (30%) — Quick tips, shortcuts, best practices
3. **Tool Reviews** (15%) — VS Code extensions, libraries, frameworks
4. **Career** (10%) — Interview tips, salary, job market
5. **Behind-the-scenes** (5%) — Setup, workflow, day in life

### Step 3: Determine Posting Schedule

**Recommended frequencies:**
- **TikTok**: Daily or 5x/week (15-60 seconds)
- **YouTube**: 2x/week (8-15 minutes)
- **Instagram**: 
  - Reels: 5x/week
  - Feed posts: 3-5x/week
  - Stories: Daily
- **Blog**: 1-2x/week (1000-2500 words)
- **Twitter**: Daily (1-3 tweets + 1-2 threads/week)

**Best posting times (WIB):**
- **TikTok**: 19:00-21:00 (peak), 12:00-13:00 (lunch)
- **YouTube**: 15:00-17:00, 19:00-21:00
- **Instagram**: 12:00-13:00, 19:00-21:00
- **Blog**: Tuesday-Thursday, 09:00-11:00
- **Twitter**: 08:00-09:00, 12:00-13:00, 19:00-21:00

### Step 5: Balance Content Types

**Content mix per week:**
- **Educational** (60%): Tutorials, how-tos, explainers
- **Engagement** (20%): Polls, Q&A, challenges
- **Promotional** (10%): Products, services, affiliate
- **Personal** (10%): Behind-the-scenes, stories, milestones

**Cross-platform repurposing:**
- YouTube video → Cut into 3-5 TikTok/Reels clips
- Blog post → Convert to Twitter thread
- TikTok → Repost as Instagram Reel (remove watermark)
- Instagram carousel → Expand into blog post

### Step 6: Add Special Dates

**Indonesian holidays & events:**
- **January**: Tahun Baru, Imlek (check lunar calendar)
- **February**: Valentine's Day (tech angle: apps for couples)
- **March**: Hari Wanita Internasional (women in tech)
- **April**: Kartini Day (inspiring Indonesian women in tech)
- **June**: Idul Fitri (check Islamic calendar)
- **August**: Independence Day (tech Indonesia yang mendunia)
- **October**: Sumpah Pemuda (developer Indonesia bersatu)
- **November**: 11.11 Harbolnas (promo content)
- **December**: 12.12 Harbolnas, Year in Review

### Step 8: Add Engagement Strategy

**Daily engagement tasks:**
- Reply to all comments within 2 hours
- Engage with 5-10 similar accounts (comment on their posts)
- Share user-generated content (with permission)
- Post Stories with interactive stickers (polls, questions)

**Weekly engagement tasks:**
- Analyze top-performing content
- Adjust next week's calendar based on data
- Plan 1 collaboration or shoutout
- Review analytics and KPIs

## Tools for Calendar Management

**Recommended tools:**
- **Notion**: Free, flexible, good for content planning
- **Trello**: Visual kanban board
- **Google Sheets**: Simple, shareable
- **Airtable**: Database-style, good for tracking
- **Pen and paper**: Sometimes the best tool!
